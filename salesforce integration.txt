As I recently started my career in salesforce, I've been curious about integrations. And addded to this I've some knowledge on backend technologies already.
Salesforce is a CRM cloud platform to manage and orchestrate the process with customer's data. Sometimes I wonder why couldn't we develop our own system or architecture and deploy it on the cloud. But I got the answer for this question as I spent time in my job.
The development cost, time, integrity, low code, admin console etc... have to be considered to create a platform like salesforce from the scratch. But here the salesforce with 20 years of engineering and architecture and with progressive mindset provides everything with a fair cost.
Which would you choose? whether building it from scratch without the assurance of integrity or using existing platform like salesforce. I hope you have got the answer too.
So this developed an urge to learn the integration for salesforce. Salesforce has exposed its every nuke and corner via endpoint with some auths which we will see further in this series. The Integration is generally about connecting two different systems to achieve some product. In salesforce there are efficient ways to connect salesforce to external application which we are going to learn in this series. Just follow up this series and we'll catch up soon.
